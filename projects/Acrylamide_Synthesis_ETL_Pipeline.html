Acrylamide synthesis ETL pipeline

Repository on GitHub ↗

From synthesis data to analysis

How do you turn records about a chemical synthesis into data you can compare? This project explores that question through an extract, transform and load (ETL) pipeline for acrylamide synthesis.

How to read the pipeline

  1. Extract. Identify the source records and what each observation represents.
  2. Transform. Put comparable observations into a consistent form. For example, two temperatures can only be compared directly once they share a scale.
  3. Load. Write the prepared records to their destination so they can be queried or analysed together.

Research context

In my related presentation on solvent design, I explore how researchers select a solvent to accelerate a reaction. The study compares reaction rate constants at 298 K and uses melting and boiling points to constrain the search. That distinction matters when reading a dataset: the rate constant is the quantity being compared, while temperature is a condition of the comparison.
