# About me

I’m Eileen Chang, a London-based UCL graduate with a BEng in Chemical Engineering and an MSc in Digital Manufacturing of Advanced Materials, awarded with Distinction. I enjoy building tools that answer practical questions, whether that means making a lab experiment repeatable or helping someone understand what drives sales.

My engineering background shapes how I approach data. I want to know how a measurement was made, what might explain an unusual result and how confidently I can draw a conclusion. Three projects give a sense of how I work.

## Selected projects

- **Customer shopping behaviour.** I cleaned retail data in Python, used PostgreSQL to compare customer segments and built a Power BI dashboard to show which products generated the most revenue and what proportion of customers had subscriptions. [Explore the project on GitHub](https://github.com/Hsuan1029/Business-Analysis)
- **Chemical research data.** For my MSc thesis, I built a Python pipeline to analyse 12,155 Reaxys records spanning 25 years. One challenge was recognising when different names referred to the same chemical. Standardising those names let me trace changes in reagent and solvent use over time. [View the pipeline on GitHub](https://github.com/Hsuan1029/Acrylamide_Synthesis_ETL_Pipeline)
- **Laboratory automation.** At UCL East Manufacturing Futures Lab, I developed a Python protocol for an Opentrons Flex robot that processed 90 sample sets in parallel. I also tested and refined a dispensing method until the mass of aspirin dispensed was within 3% of its target across repeated trials.

## Beyond the code

During my internship with the Energy & Climate Intelligence Unit, I modelled the potential costs of UK housing policy in Excel and used Mandarin to review Taiwanese companies’ progress towards net zero. I also enjoy sharing technical work. After co-developing an Opentrons simulator, I presented our work at the 53rd SEFI Annual Conference in Finland.
